How can we help?

Quick answers to the most common questions about SkillShare.

Is SkillShare free to use?

Yes. SkillShare is built for peer-to-peer learning. You can explore skills, request sessions, and share your own knowledge without a platform fee.

How do I request a session?

Open a tutor’s public profile, choose the skill you want to learn, add an optional note, and send the request. The tutor can accept, decline, or suggest another time.

How do I become a tutor?

Complete your profile and create a teaching session. Add a clear title, learner level, description, and availability so other members can find you.

What should I do if I feel unsafe or receive spam?

Stop the conversation and contact support. We will review reports and can restrict accounts that breach our community expectations.

Can I change or cancel a session?

Yes. Use your calendar or requests page to manage sessions. Please give the other person as much notice as possible.
