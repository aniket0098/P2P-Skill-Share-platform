Community safety

SkillShare works best when every learner and teacher feels respected, comfortable, and in control.

Keep conversations respectful

Be kind, honest, and constructive. Harassment, hate speech, unwanted messages, scams, and impersonation are not allowed.

Protect your privacy

Use SkillShare messages until you feel comfortable. Never share passwords, one-time codes, banking details, or other sensitive data in a session or message.

Meet and learn safely

Use the platform session tools where possible. If you arrange an in-person session, choose a public location and tell someone you trust about your plans.

Report a concern

If a member breaks these rules or you feel unsafe, stop engaging and contact our support team. We review reports and may restrict accounts to protect the community.
