Credit Questions

Everything you need to know about SkillShare credits.

How do I earn credits?

You earn credits through eligible activities such as completed Live Learning sessions, positive reviews from learners, unlocked achievements and consistent participation on the platform.

How do reviews affect credits?

After an eligible Live Learning session, your learner can leave a review. Higher-rated reviews earn more: a 5-star review gives +50 credits, 4-star gives +30 and 3-star gives +10. Reviews below 3 stars do not earn credits.

Can I buy credits?

Yes — credit packages are shown above. Purchasing will become available once payment functionality is connected to SkillShare. Until then, packages are displayed for preview only.

Can credits be converted into money?

No. SkillShare credits are an internal learning currency. They can be used for sessions and platform features but cannot be exchanged or withdrawn as money.

Do credits expire?

Credit expiry rules have not been defined yet by the SkillShare team. If an expiry policy is introduced, it will be announced on this page before it takes effect.
