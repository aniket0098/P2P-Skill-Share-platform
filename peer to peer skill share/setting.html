Security & login

How your account is protected. Sessions are stateless tokens — signing out ends access on this device immediately.

Security status

  • Password set

    Your password is stored as a bcrypt hash — never visible to anyone, including us.

  • Account active

    Signed in with a JWT session that stays valid until it expires or you log out.

  • No device list available

    CareerBridge sessions are stateless — there is no server-side device registry to show, so none is displayed here.

Change password

Session

Data & account

Take your data with you and understand exactly what SkillShare stores.

Export your data

Download a JSON copy of everything tied to your account:

  • Profile and account fields (never your password)
  • Your settings and privacy choices
  • Education, skills, projects and activity history
  • Notification counts by category

Message threads with other people are intentionally excluded so their content is never exported through your account.

How your data is used

  • Your profile fields power search, discovery and opportunity matching.
  • Privacy choices in this Settings page are enforced server-side.
  • Notifications stay in your account until you read or they are pruned.
  • Appearance and accessibility choices never leave this device.

Questions? See the Privacy Policy and Safety Center.

Danger zone

Actions here affect your whole account. Read each description carefully.

Deactivate account

Temporarily leave the platform. You disappear from discovery and collaboration suggestions, and your account is flagged until you reactivate by signing in and returning here. Fully reversible.

Delete account

Permanently erase and anonymize your personal data: name, email, password, profile and settings. Projects, messages and applications you created remain for other members' integrity but display as “Deleted user”. This cannot be undone and you will not be able to sign in again.

Deactivate your account

You will disappear from search, discovery and collaboration suggestions. Nothing is deleted. Sign in again any time and reactivate from Settings.

Delete your account

This permanently erases and anonymizes your personal data. It cannot be undone.

  • Your name, email, password and profile are destroyed.
  • You sign out everywhere and cannot log in again.
  • Projects, messages and applications you created stay for other members, de-identified as “Deleted user”.
  • Your settings and notifications are removed.
