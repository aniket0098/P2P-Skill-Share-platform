Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

We collect the information you provide to create and use your SkillShare profile, such as your name, bio, skills, session requests, and messages. We use it to operate the service and help members connect safely.

How we use information

Your public profile information helps other learners discover relevant skills. Private account details are used only to operate, secure, and improve SkillShare.

Your choices

You can update profile visibility and notification preferences in Settings. You can also request access to or deletion of your account information by contacting support.

Safety

We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee complete security. Do not share passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information in messages.
